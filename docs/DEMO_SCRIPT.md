# Relay — 90 second walkthrough

1. **Start in the inbox**
   - Search and filter the queue.
   - Open Adaeze's urgent repeat-delivery ticket.
   - Point out the conversation, customer history and Copilot living together.

2. **Show grounded AI**
   - Review the 93% confidence draft.
   - Open the Delayed Delivery Playbook source.
   - Explain that the suggestion cites the policy it used and why it made the recommendation.

3. **Show uncertainty**
   - Open Tobi's renewal ticket.
   - The confidence drops because post-renewal usage is not known.
   - Open Approve & send and show the explicit review confirmation required before sending.

4. **Show streaming generation**
   - Open Mariam's address-change ticket.
   - Generate a response.
   - Watch Relay retrieve knowledge, check customer history and stream the draft.

5. **Show the rest of the product**
   - Open Knowledge and create/edit an article.
   - Open Customers and update context.
   - Open Analytics to show queue health and human review outcomes.
   - Use Ctrl/Cmd + K to jump back to a ticket.

6. **Close with the principle**
   - Relay does not auto-send AI output.
   - The system is designed around a reviewable decision boundary: AI proposes; the agent decides.
